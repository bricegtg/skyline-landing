The Machine

MEET THE MOWER.

This remote-control tracked mower is built for ground that stops most machines: tall grass, dense weeds, embankments and slopes. The operator stays back with the handheld remote control while the mower works the ground on its rubber tracks.

Skyline offers the mower two ways. Hire us to mow your site as a service, or buy the mower outright and run it yourself — it's available now.

It's a ground robot, not an aircraft: no one rides it, and no one needs to walk the slope it's cutting.

Built For Difficult Ground

Suited for ground too steep, too thick or too awkward for a push-mower or a ride-on — embankments and slopes, vacant lots, large lawns, roadside verges, estates and golf roughs.
