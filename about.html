The Company

BUILT FOR EVERY HEIGHT.

Skyline Drones was founded in Manila to solve a single, stubborn problem — the human cost of working at height. In the Philippines, facade cleaning, fire response, and aerial inspection all still rely on workers suspended from ropes, balanced on scaffolding, or hanging from helicopter skids. Each of those is a death waiting to happen, and every operator who has done this work long enough has seen it.

We replaced the worker, not the work. Three commercial drone divisions, one CAAP-certified pilot crew, one operations and insurance umbrella. The result is faster turnaround, lower liability, and zero workers leaving the ground.

We operate across Metro Manila first, and nationwide on request. Every flight is logged, every airspace clearance is filed, and every job is documented end-to-end.

One Principle

Every job at height should be done by a machine, not a human. We engineer each division around that single commitment — and the safety, insurance, and documentation that make it real.
