Why Makati Buildings Choose Drone Cleaning

Makati is home to some of Metro Manila's tallest and most prestigious commercial real estate — PBCom Tower, GT Tower, Zuellig, RCBC Plaza, The Peninsula Manila, Makati Shangri-La, Greenbelt, Glorietta, and Rockwell Center. These towers face a common problem: building cleaning Makati by traditional rope access is slow, expensive, and exposes workers to fall risks at height. Skyline Drones solves all three.

Our CAAP-certified drone pilots clean facades up to 160 metres using deionised water with software-controlled spray pressure. A typical 20-storey Makati office tower is cleaned in 2–4 days — versus 3–4 weeks with rope access. Zero workers at height. Zero scaffolding. Zero sidewalk closures.

We serve every major building category in Makati: Ayala Avenue Grade-A towers, Salcedo and Legazpi village offices, Rockwell mixed-use, premium hotels, hospitals, and embassies. Whether you manage a single Grade-A office tower or a portfolio of mixed-use developments, our drone fleet, pilots, and insurance coverage scale to your needs.
