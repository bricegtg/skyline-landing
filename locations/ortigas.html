Why Ortigas Buildings Choose Drone Cleaning

Ortigas is home to some of Metro Manila's tallest and most prestigious commercial real estate — Robinsons Galleria, SM Megamall, Shangri-La Plaza, Podium, San Miguel HQ, Asian Development Bank, Tektite Towers, and Ortigas Center corridor. These towers face a common problem: high rise cleaning Ortigas by traditional rope access is slow, expensive, and exposes workers to fall risks at height. Skyline Drones solves all three.

Our CAAP-certified drone pilots clean facades up to 160 metres using deionised water with software-controlled spray pressure. A typical 20-storey Ortigas office tower is cleaned in 2–4 days — versus 3–4 weeks with rope access. Zero workers at height. Zero scaffolding. Zero sidewalk closures.

We serve every major building category in Ortigas: office towers, mixed-use developments, malls, hospitals, condominiums, and corporate headquarters. Whether you manage a single Grade-A office tower or a portfolio of mixed-use developments, our drone fleet, pilots, and insurance coverage scale to your needs.
