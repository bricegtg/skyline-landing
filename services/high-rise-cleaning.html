THE PROBLEM

High-Rise Cleaning Has Always Been Dangerous & Slow

Traditional high-rise cleaning in the Philippines means rope-access crews dangling from rooftops, gondolas suspended on cables, or scaffolding wrapped around an entire tower for weeks. Property managers face a three-way trade-off: cost, safety, and disruption — pick two.

Skyline Drones changes that math. A single tethered drone, operated from the ground by a CAAP-certified pilot, cleans a 20-storey tower in 1–2 days. No scaffolding. No rope teams. No worker fatigue at altitude. The pilot's hands stay on the controller. The water stays under pressure. The job stays on schedule.
