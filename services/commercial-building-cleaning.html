OVERVIEW

The Modern Way to Clean a Commercial Building

TL;DR — Skyline Drones is a CAAP-certified commercial drone operator providing exterior building cleaning across the Philippines. We clean office towers, malls, hotels, condominiums, and industrial facilities up to 160 metres tall — typically 5–10× faster than rope access, with zero workers at height and fully insured operations.

Commercial building cleaning in the Philippines has historically meant scaffolding, gondolas, or rope-access crews — slow, expensive, and dangerous. Skyline Drones replaces those methods with a tethered drone system that delivers continuous water and detergent to a precision nozzle at altitude, operated by a CAAP-certified pilot from the ground.

We serve property managers, building owners, facility management companies, hotel groups, mall operators, and industrial estates across Metro Manila and the rest of the Philippines. Our crew is fully insured, operates under documented method statements, and provides building managers with a complete safety pack before every job.

FAQS

Commercial Building Cleaning FAQs

How much does commercial building cleaning cost in the Philippines?

Costs typically range from ₱40 to ₱180 per square metre depending on building height, facade material, and access difficulty. Drone-powered cleaning is quoted as a fixed fee per job — we provide a written quote within 48 hours of receiving building photos and address.

How is drone building cleaning different from traditional methods?

Drone cleaning eliminates scaffolding, gondolas, and rope access. A single CAAP-certified pilot operates a tethered drone from the ground, completing a job 5–10× faster than rope-access crews with zero workers at height. Suitable for buildings 30–160 metres on any facade material.

What types of commercial buildings do you clean?

Office towers, condominiums, hotels, malls, hospitals, government buildings, schools, industrial facilities, and warehouses across Metro Manila and the rest of the Philippines.

Is drone building cleaning safe?

Yes. Skyline Drones is CAAP-certified, fully insured, and operates under written method statements and risk assessments for every job. Because no human leaves the ground, fall-from-height risk — the leading cause of building maintenance fatalities — is eliminated.

How long does a commercial building cleaning job take?

A 20-storey tower typically takes 1–2 days with drone cleaning, versus 5–14 days for scaffolding-based methods. Schedule depends on weather windows, building access permits, and tenant coordination.
