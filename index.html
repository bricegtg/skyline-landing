The Company

BUILT FOR EVERY HEIGHT.

Skyline Drones was founded in Manila to solve a single problem — the human cost of working at height. In the Philippines, facade cleaning, fire response, and aerial inspection all still rely on workers suspended from ropes, balanced on scaffolding, or hanging from helicopter skids. Each of those is a death waiting to happen, and every operator who has done this work long enough has seen it.

We replaced the worker, not the work. Three commercial drone divisions, one CAAP-certified pilot crew, one operations and insurance umbrella. The result is faster turnaround, lower liability, and zero workers leaving the ground.

We operate across Metro Manila first, and nationwide on request. Every flight is logged, every airspace clearance is filed, and every job is documented end-to-end.

One Discipline

One discipline runs across every division: the same certified pilots, the same tested aircraft, the same insurance umbrella, the same documentation standard — whether we're cleaning a 50-story tower, fighting an industrial fire, or capturing aerial imagery.
