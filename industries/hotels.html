DRONE FACADE CLEANING FOR HOTELS

Hotel Facade Cleaning in the Philippines — Without Disturbing Guests

Hotel general managers across Metro Manila face a recurring problem: facade cleaning by rope access takes 3–4 weeks, disrupts guest views, occupies sidewalks, and forces difficult conversations with VIP guests whose windows are temporarily blocked by harnessed workers. Skyline Drones eliminates all of that.

Our drone facade cleaning crews complete a typical 20-storey hotel facade in 2–4 days. Flights are scheduled during low-occupancy windows — typically mornings between 9 AM and noon. There is no scaffolding to obstruct guest room views. No harnessed workers passing guest windows. No extended occupancy of the building exterior. Just clean glass and a fresh facade, delivered fast.

We currently serve 5-star, 4-star, and boutique hotels across BGC, Makati, Ortigas, Pasay, and the Manila Bay corridor. Engagements typically begin with a single facade-cleaning trial and grow into recurring service contracts (quarterly or semi-annually).

HOTEL FAQ

Common Questions from Hotel Operators

How quickly can you mobilise? For Metro Manila hotels, we can mobilise within 7 business days of contract signing — pending weather and CAAP flight permit (typically 3 business days).
