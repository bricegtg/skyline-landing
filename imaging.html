Why Not a Crane or Helicopter

ALTITUDE IS AN ANGLE NO CRANE CAN REACH.

Constraint 01

Too High for a Jib

The decisive frame almost always sits between thirty and one hundred and sixty meters — well above the reach of any crane or jib. The establishing shot lives in air a ground rig simply cannot put a camera into.

Constraint 02

Too Expensive for a Helicopter

That same band is too low and too costly for a manned helicopter. Charter rates, mobilization, and airspace overhead price most projects out of the aerial they actually need. A drone lives precisely in that gap.

Constraint 03

Impossible from the Street

From the ground, the context, the scale, and the geometry of a project never quite resolve. Lift the camera into the gap a drone owns and the establishing shot, the context, and the scale of the project finally make sense.
